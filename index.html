Employee Leave Record 2026

A leave tracker for around 180 employees, built from scratch for HR and designed so whoever inherits it can keep it running without me.

  • Yellow cells are typed by hand, grey cells calculate themselves. The colour coding carries the instructions: if it is grey, do not type in it.
  • Years of service derive from the join date, annual and sick leave balances update as days are entered, and hospitalisation is held to its 60-day ceiling.
  • A dashboard sheet counts anyone over their entitlement and flags rows still missing a join date, so mistakes surface instead of sitting unnoticed in a file of 180 rows.
  • Designations live on their own sheet and feed a drop-down, so HR can add a job title without touching a formula, and an instructions sheet up front explains the rest.

FFB Collecting Budget Workbook

The budget template I inherited had drifted out of shape, so I rebuilt it into a single sheet that reconciles, then made the software read from it.

  • Three defects in the original: one sheet was titled for one collecting centre but held another centre’s figures, a second repeated a third centre’s numbers, and the per-tonne columns referenced the wrong cells, reporting a few sen per tonne where the real cost ran to tens of ringgit.
  • Rebuilt as one sheet covering three collecting centres, fifteen cost categories with the transport breakdown nested under its parent line, and five financial years carrying both forecast and actual columns.
  • The grand total counts numbered lines only, so a sub-item is never double counted against the parent it belongs to.
  • It became the data source the budget application imports, and the import script re-checks every site and year against the sheet’s own totals and refuses to run if they disagree.

Excel · openpyxl · Data cleaning
