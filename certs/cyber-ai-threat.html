Cybersecurity & AI Threat Awareness

Wisdom Sanctuary · 22 July 2026 · National Training Week 2026

Recognising newer AI-assisted threats such as deepfakes and smarter scams.

Generative tools have removed the old tells. Poor grammar and clumsy formatting used to mark a scam; synthesised voice and video now clear that bar easily.

The defence shifts from spotting mistakes to verifying through a second channel, because the message itself can no longer be trusted on appearance.
