GoogleSheet: AppSheet

ADTEC JTM Kampus Selandar · 23 July 2026 · National Training Week 2026

Turning a spreadsheet into a working mobile app with no code.

AppSheet reads a Google Sheet and generates a mobile app on top of it, with forms, filters and offline capture. The sheet stays the database.

Useful where a team already lives in a spreadsheet and needs data captured in the field, without funding a full application build.
