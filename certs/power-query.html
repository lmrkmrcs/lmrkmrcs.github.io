R.O.C.K. Your Data with Power Query

WhyExcel PLT · 18 July 2026 · National Training Week 2026

Cleaning and reshaping messy spreadsheets so the same steps re-run on next month’s file.

Power Query records cleaning as a series of steps instead of manual edits. Point it at next month's file and every step replays, which is the difference between fixing a spreadsheet once and fixing it forever.

This matched a real problem from my internship, where supplier data arrived in four different shapes from four collecting centres and had to be reconciled every month.
